# Rules in short — Cloud Native Ayacucho Community (CNAYP) · English

A condensed version of the server rules, meant to be read in a minute: a single Discord message.
**It does not replace the full rules** in [`community-rules.en.md`](community-rules.en.md) — those
are still the ones that govern — it summarises them and points back to them. The Spanish version
lives in [`community-rules-short.es.md`](community-rules-short.es.md); **the two must say the same
thing**, so any change here is mirrored there in the same commit. And if you change a rule in the
full version, check that this summary still tells it right.

**Where to use it.** As a welcome message, as the first message of the rules channel, or anywhere
four messages is too many. It fits in a normal Discord message (under 2000 characters). Only markdown
that Discord actually renders is used (a `#` header, bold and bare URLs). When pasting, type
`#rules` and pick the channel from the autocomplete so it becomes a real link; pasted as-is it is
plain text.

This file is the source of truth for the summary: edit here, then re-post.

```
# 📜 Rules — Cloud Native Ayacucho Community (CNAYP)

We are a volunteer community that learns and shares knowledge about cloud native technologies. By taking part you agree to these rules and to the CNCF Code of Conduct.

1️⃣ **Respect comes first.** Empathy, kindness and inclusive language. Constructive criticism is welcome; personal attacks are not.
2️⃣ **We are here to learn and to share.** There are no stupid questions. Ask with context, share the answer once you find it, and correct people kindly.
3️⃣ **Keep channels on topic.** Post in the right channel, take long conversations into a thread, and no flooding or @everyone / @here.
4️⃣ **Spanish and English are both welcome.**
5️⃣ **No spam, no self-promotion.** Your blog, meetup or job opening: once, in the right channel. No mass DMs and no recruiting by DM without asking first.
6️⃣ **No exam dumps, no pirated material.** Not CKA, CKAD, CKS, KCNA or any other certification; no cracks or shared accounts either.
7️⃣ **Protect privacy.** Don't publish other people's information, and check what you paste: tokens, kubeconfigs and logs routinely carry secrets.
8️⃣ **No scams.** No crypto, no airdrops, no "opportunities". No moderator will ever ask you for passwords, tokens or payments by DM.
9️⃣ **Follow Discord's rules.** At least 13 years old, one account per person, and no NSFW, hateful or illegal content.

🛟 **Something making you uncomfortable?** DM anyone with the **Moderator** role, or email kenesparta@pm.me · glisselisbeth@gmail.com. Reports are confidential and there is never retaliation.

⚖️ Depending on severity and repetition: private warning → temporary mute → removal → ban.

📖 This is the short version. The full rules (unacceptable behaviour, how to report, enforcement) are in #rules and at:
https://github.com/kenesparta/cnayp-discord-bot/blob/main/docs/community-rules.en.md
```
