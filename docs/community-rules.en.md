# Rules — Cloud Native Ayacucho Community (CNAYP) · English

Source text for the server's `#rules` channel. The Spanish version lives in
[`community-rules.es.md`](community-rules.es.md); **the two must say the same thing**, so any change
here is mirrored there in the same commit.

Based on the [CNCF Community Code of Conduct v1.3](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md)
— the expected and unacceptable behaviours follow its wording — plus the
[Discord Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) and the conventions of a technical
learning community.

**How to post this.** Discord caps a normal message at 2000 characters, so the text is split into
four messages. Copy each fenced block and send it as its own message, in order. Only markdown that
Discord actually renders is used (`#` headers, `-#` subtext, bold, lists, blockquotes, bare URLs —
`[text](url)` links do not render in messages sent by a person). Post them in a channel where
`@everyone` cannot write, then set that channel as the server's Rules Channel in
**Server Settings → Community → Rules**.

This file is the source of truth: edit here, then re-post. If you change the rules, announce it in
the server — the last line promises exactly that — and check that the one-message summary in
[`community-rules-short.en.md`](community-rules-short.en.md) still tells them right. What Discord
shows to someone joining — the welcome sign, the new-member to-dos and the condensed rules of the
Rules Screening — is in [`onboarding.es.md`](onboarding.es.md), Spanish only, since Discord holds a
single welcome sign and a single rule set per server.

```
# 📜 Rules — Cloud Native Ayacucho Community (CNAYP)

Welcome! We are a volunteer community that learns and shares knowledge about cloud native technologies. This server is **educational and informational**: we are here to learn, to share what we know with the community, and to help other people learn.

By taking part you agree to these rules and to the **CNCF Code of Conduct** (v1.3):
https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md

## 1️⃣ Respect comes first

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully when it is given to you.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not just for yourself.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.

## 2️⃣ This is a place to learn

- There are no stupid questions. Everyone started somewhere.
- Don't ask "does anyone know Kubernetes?". Ask your actual question, with context: what you are trying to do, which version you are on, the full error, and what you already tried.
- Share the answer once you find it. The next person with your problem will thank you.
- Nobody owes you a reply. Be patient, and don't bump your question with mentions.
- Correct people kindly. Nobody here mocks someone for learning.
```

```
## 3️⃣ Keep channels on topic

- Post in the channel that fits. If you are not sure which one, ask.
- Take long conversations into a thread so the channel stays readable.
- No flooding, no all-caps, no repeated messages.
- Don't use @everyone or @here, and don't ping the moderators unless it is genuinely urgent.

## 4️⃣ Spanish and English are both welcome

- Write in whichever language you are comfortable with. Most of us write in Spanish.
- Much cloud native documentation is English-only: ask if you need help understanding something. No one will judge you for it.

## 5️⃣ No spam, no self-promotion

- No mass DMs and no unsolicited advertising.
- Have a blog, a meetup, a project, a job opening? Post it once in the right channel and stay for the conversation. Posting and vanishing is spam.
- Recruiting by DM without asking first is not allowed.
- No referral links, and no invites to other servers without a moderator's approval.

## 6️⃣ No exam dumps, no pirated material

- Asking for, offering, or sharing **dumps** or real questions from CKA, CKAD, CKS, KCNA or any other certification is forbidden. It breaks the Linux Foundation certification agreement and can cost you your certification.
- The same goes for pirated software, cracks, and shared accounts for paid platforms.
- Studying together, yes. Copying the exam, no.
```

```
## 7️⃣ Protect privacy — yours and everyone else's

- Never publish anyone's personal information without their explicit permission, and don't screenshot private conversations.
- Check what you paste before you send it: tokens, kubeconfigs, access keys, internal IPs and logs routinely carry secrets.
- If you leak a credential by accident, rotate it immediately and tell a moderator. Nobody here will scold you for it.

## 8️⃣ No scams, no "opportunities"

- No promoting crypto, airdrops, NFTs, trading signals, gambling, or investment schemes.
- Be suspicious of job offers by DM that ask you for a payment, your bank details, or a "test" that involves moving money. Report them and don't reply.
- No moderator will ever ask you for your password, your token, or a payment by DM.
```

```
## 🚫 Unacceptable behaviour

Taken from the CNCF Code of Conduct. The following are not allowed in this server:

- the use of sexualised language or imagery;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment in any form;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission;
- violence, threatening violence, or encouraging others to engage in violent behaviour;
- stalking or following someone without their consent;
- unwelcome physical contact;
- unwelcome sexual or romantic attention or advances;
- misusing community spaces for unrelated political campaigning;
- knowingly providing false or misleading information during an investigation;
- retaliating against a person because they reported an incident.

## 🛟 How to report

If something makes you uncomfortable, or you see one of these rules being broken:

1. **In the server** — DM anyone with the **Moderator** role.
2. **By email**, if you would rather not report by DM — Ken: kenesparta@pm.me · Glisse: glisselisbeth@gmail.com. If your report is about one of them, write to the other.
3. **To the CNCF** — if it involves a CNCF project or event: conduct@cncf.io · eventconduct@cncf.io

Reports are handled confidentially, and you will get a response within **three business days**. There will **never** be retaliation for reporting in good faith.

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Depending on severity and repetition: private warning → temporary mute → removal → permanent ban. Serious cases — threats, harassment, doxxing — can go straight to a ban.

The moderation team's decision is final. If you believe it was a mistake, write to kenesparta@pm.me and we will review it.

-# These rules may change. Material changes will be announced in the server.
```
